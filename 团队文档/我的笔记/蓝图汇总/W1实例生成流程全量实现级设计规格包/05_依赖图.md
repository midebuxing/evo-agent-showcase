## 2. 段内字段流（Step 1 ↔ Step 2-8）

Step 1 产出的字段被 Step 2-8 消费：

| Step 1 输出字段 | 被 Step 2-8 哪段消费 | 用途 |
|---|---|---|
| `BuildingContext` | Step 9 | 派生 building-level risk / repair flag |
| `ComponentNode[]` | Step 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 8 | 各 mechanism 派生需要 component 锚点 |
| `LocationNode[]` | Step 2 / 3 | 暴露 / 位置作为 driver 输入 |
| `FragmentContext[]` | Step 2 / 6 / 7 | mechanism 激活 + measurement 派生入口 |
| `CoverageRelation[]` | Step 6 | coverage ratio 派生输入（按 W0 `coverage_relation_registry`）|
| `DriverState[]` | Step 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 | 所有 mechanism 激活 + severity 公式输入 |

## 3. 段间数据流（Step 2-5 ↔ Step 6-8）

| Step 2-5 输出 | 被 Step 6-8 哪段消费 |
|---|---|
| MechanismState[] / ConditionState[] | Step 6 (coverage area)；Step 7 (technical validation 输入 condition)；Step 8 (FSP 输入 condition severity) |
| DrainageState[] | Step 6 (drainage 相关 coverage / sampling) |
| UBWState[] | Step 6 (UBW alteration area)；Step 9 (UBW derived risk flag) |
| FireSafetyState[] | Step 6 (fire-door inspection sampling)；Step 9 (fire-safety derived risk flag) |

## 4. Step 9 派生 flag 依赖

Step 9 派生 flag 的输入来源（详见 `08_派生flag.md`）：

| Derived flag | 输入 |
|---|---|
| `risk.building_safety.emergency` | ConditionState (DC_CRACK / DC_SPALL_REBAR / DC_HOLLOWING / DC_DETACHMENT / DC_DEFORMATION_DISPLACEMENT) + structural severity |
| `risk.public_health.emergency` | DrainageState (DC_DRAINAGE_LEAKAGE / DC_DRAINAGE_MISCONNECTION / DC_DRAINAGE_BLOCKAGE) |
| `risk.public_danger.present` | ConditionState (DC_DETACHMENT / DC_SPALL_REBAR / DC_GLASS_BREAKAGE) + UBWState + FireSafetyState (DC_FIRE_DOOR_DEFICIENCY) |
| `repair.required` | risk.building_safety.emergency + risk.public_danger.present + verification.test.failed |
| `repair.outcome.safe_until_next_cycle` | risk.building_safety.emergency + verification.test.failed |
| `maintenance.pre_next_cycle.required` | 无 risk 输入（routine）|
| `investigation.fsp.below_required_safety` | FSP measurement < floor_proxy（spec 06 §11.X derived flag formula）|
| `verification.test.failed` | technical_validation measurement vs `verification_test_registry.failure_rule` |
| `coverage.insufficient` | covered / inspected ratios < `coverage_floor_proxy=0.35`（DEBT-013 closure）|

## 5. sidecar 派生层依赖

sidecar 派生层（spec 09 §1.2 双路径）跟九段并行：

```text
W1 worldgen core 物理 state
        │
        ▼
sidecar 派生层
   ├── sidecar measurement（duration / interval 等）
   │     └── W0 spec 06 §13/§14 噪声 + DEBT-020 Round 3/5/7 distribution
   │
   └── sidecar bool / categorical
         └── W0 spec 06 §11.6 conditional formula evaluator
         └── W0 spec 06 §12 BERNOULLI / MULTINOMIAL prevalence sampler
         └── DEBT-020 Round 4 marginal prevalence + Round 6/7 anchor 修订
```

sidecar 派生层 **只读** W1 worldgen core 物理 state；**不读** rule_card / projection_registry（rule-blind 红线）。
